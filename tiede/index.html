    1. Introduction and Problem Formulation

    An autoregressive language model is formally defined as a conditional probability distribution over a discrete vocabulary $\mathcal{V}$:

    $$P(Y \mid X; \theta) = \prod_{t=1}^{T} P(y_t \mid y_{<t}, X; \theta), \quad y_t \in \mathcal{V} \tag{1}$$

    where $X = (x_1, \dots, x_N)$ is an input prompt sequence and $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^D$ represents the static model weights. Although modern frontier models produce syntactically fluent discourse, their epistemic utility in high-stakes reasoning is bounded by three structural failures:

    1. Aleatoric Uncertainty and Calibration Divergence: The distribution $P(Y \mid X; \theta)$ models statistical token co-occurrence frequencies across training corpora rather than objective truth values ($\mathcal{T} \neq \mathcal{P}$). The model generates statistically plausible assertions that lack empirical backing.
    2. Conflation of Association with Causation: Autoregressive attention captures symmetric correlation $P(A \mid B)$ but cannot distinguish a genuine causal mechanism $B \to A$ from confounding background factors ($C \to A \land C \to B$) or reverse causation ($A \to B$).
    3. Superlinear Error Cascade: In unconstrained multi-step inference, a minute semantic drift at step $t$ conditions the distribution at step $t+1$, causing errors to compound exponentially across generative trajectories—formalizing exposure bias and compositional error cascades in autoregressive models (Ross & Bagnell, 2010; Dziri et al., 2023):

    $$\mathbb{E}\left[|\hat{y}{t+k} - y^*{t+k}|\right] \sim \mathcal{O}\left(e^{\lambda_{\text{casc}} k}\right), \quad \lambda_{\text{casc}} > 0 \tag{2}$$

    The Quorum architecture addresses these vulnerabilities by stripping the LLM of autonomous state transitions. The generative model is restricted to a stateless, constrained propositional sensor, while the control logic, state progression, and evidentiary evaluation are enforced by an external, mathematically validated neurosymbolic engine.

However, deterministic string matching is semantically blind: it verifies the syntactic existence of character sequences, not that proposition $\tau_i$ semantically follows from $\mathcal{C}_i$ ($\mathcal{C}_i \models \tau_i$). To isolate the generative sensor from persistent storage mechanics, the neurosymbolic boundary executes an ephemeral bijective alias morphism:

    $$\mu: \mathcal{I}{\text{UUID}} \longleftrightarrow \mathcal{V}{\text{alias}}, \quad \mathcal{V}{\text{alias}} = {a_0, a_1, \dots, a{n-1}}$$

    4.1.1. Deterministic Physical Provenance Verification and Defense Against Fabricated Evidence

    A foundational failure mode in contemporary LLM evaluation (including LLM-as-a-Judge [Zheng et al., 2023], G-Eval [Liu et al., 2023], and Self-RAG [Asai et al., 2024]) is Second-Order Hallucination (Quis custodiet ipsos custodes?). If a secondary LLM is queried to determine whether a document entails a proposition ($P(\text{Entail} \mid \mathcal{D}, \tau_i)$), the verifier is itself an autoregressive stochastic sampler susceptible to sycophancy, confirmation bias, and the synthesis of chimera quotes (stitching isolated words across disparate paragraphs into a fabricated sentence).

    To defend against unanchored evidence and citation fabrications, Quorum strips the generative model of judicial authority. Rather than allowing the LLM to self-certify its outputs, evidence verification is structured across two orthogonal layers: physical citation provenance and exact lexical containment are verified exclusively through a compiled deterministic lexical verification engine, while semantic entailment is audited through role-asymmetric Popperian falsification and downstream A-SCM reachability:

    1. An invariant bidirectional index map $\mathcal{M}{\text{idx}}: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ maps normalized character indices back to original source byte positions. The candidate quote $\mathcal{C}_i$ is validated via exact $O(N)$ containment:

    $$\text{find}(\text{Norm}{\text{NFKC}}(\mathcal{C}_i), \text{Norm}{\text{NFKC}}(\mathcal{D})) \neq -1$$

    If matched, the exact verbatim string is sliced from the original immutable text, preventing case-folding or formatting drifts from fabricating false evidence. This two-tier normalization design ensures that typographical variations (such as non-breaking spaces U+00A0 or en-spaces U+2002) do not induce false negative citation rejections during post-hoc auditing.

    1. Gate 2: Entropy Gate & Contiguity Guard (Chimera Quote Defense):
    2. Entropy Gate (< 10 chars): Short quotes under 10 characters (specifically isolated buzzwords) are strictly forbidden from approximate matching; 100% exact substring containment is unconditionally required.
    3. Contiguity Guard (< 30 chars): Short phrases enforce strict contiguous character sequence matching via partial string edit ratio, strictly prohibiting token reordering or word skipping.
    4. Token-Set Evaluation ($\ge 30$ chars): Longer spans evaluate token-set overlap to tolerate minor word-order permutations or morphological case conjugations. However, to prevent bag-of-words chimera stitching across disparate paragraphs, token-set overlap does not confer standalone acceptance: it is strictly constrained by Gate 5's downstream morphological stem-sequence alignment and unbroken longest-common-substring coverage verification.

    5. Safety Ceiling (> 1000 chars): Quotes exceeding 1000 characters trigger an immediate verification rejection to prevent prompt injection and quote stuffing.

    6. Gate 3: Cross-Speaker Provenance Boundary Guard: Discourse is segmented into structured provenance capsules: <user_payload> for human supervisor inputs, <ai_draft_context> for model drafts, and <assignment_context> for task instructions. When evaluating human capability ($\sigma_i = \text{USER}$), candidate quotes must reside strictly inside <user_payload>:

    $$\text{Prov}(\mathcal{C}i, \sigma_i) = \mathbb{I}\left( \text{Lex}(\mathcal{C}_i, \mathcal{D}{\text{speaker}}; \zeta) = 1 \right)$$

    If an LLM quotes the AI assistant's draft to substantiate a human competence claim, the verifier rejects the claim on provenance boundary violation, mathematically preventing cross-speaker competence contamination.

    1. Gate 4: Reasoning Trace Triangulation: The model's structured schema requires an explicit reasoning trace $\mathcal{T}_i$ specifying an exact syntactic anchor ([2. SYNTACTIC ANCHOR: "..."]) and decision ([5. VALIDATION DECISION: PASS/FAIL]). Deterministic triangulation asserts three consistency invariants:
    2. Contradiction Ban: If $\mathcal{T}_i$ concludes FAIL, but quote $\mathcal{C}_i$ is populated, the atom is rejected for logical contradiction.
    3. Empty Anchor Ban: If $\mathcal{T}_i$ declares [2. SYNTACTIC ANCHOR: NONE], unanchored extraction fails closed.

    4. Hallucinated Anchor Ban: The anchor string parsed from the trace must physically exist in the source document; fabricated anchors trigger immediate rejection.

    4.2. Argumentative Structural Dependency Models (A-SCM) and Truth Maintenance

    Ontological Scope and the Enthymeme Boundary: Unlike physical systems where variables $\mathbf{V}$ represent empirical data-generating mechanisms, natural discourse is inherently pragmatic, elliptical, and enthymematic (Toulmin, 1958; Grice, 1975). Human authors routinely omit common-sense warrants and intermediate mechanisms, while language models trained on autoregressive prediction behave as "causal parrots" conflating associative correlation with causation (Zečević et al., 2023). Therefore, Quorum does not claim that textual analysis discovers objective physical causality. Instead, Quorum formalizes the Argumentative Structural Causal Model (A-SCM), evaluating the internal explanatory sufficiency and structural evidentiary rigor of the authored thesis.

    Definition 2 (Argumentative Structural Causal Model). An authored discourse is modeled as a five-tuple:

    $$\mathcal{M}_{\text{arg}} = \langle \mathbf{U}, \mathbf{V}, \mathbf{W}, \mathbf{F}, P(\mathbf{U}) \rangle \tag{11}$$

    where $\mathbf{V} = {V_1, \dots, V_n}$ represents explicit propositional claims grounded in verbatim text ($\lambda = 1$), $\mathbf{W}$ represents explicit Toulmin warrants (backing and explanatory transmission mechanisms), $\mathbf{U} = {U_{\text{enthymeme}}, U_{\text{context}}}$ represents unstated background assumptions and latent conversational confounders, and $\mathbf{F} = {f_1, \dots, f_m}$ defines structural argument dependencies:

    $$V_i = f_i(\mathbf{PA}_i, \mathbf{W}_i, U_i), \quad \mathbf{PA}_i \subseteq \mathbf{V} \setminus { V_i } \tag{12}$$

    Definition 3 (Context-Bounded Sliding-Window Proposition Linking, $\Phi_{\text{link}}$). Constructing the Argument DAG $\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}} = (\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}, \mathcal{E}{\text{deps}})$ directly from unconstrained discourse via exhaustive pairwise comparison requires an intractable $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ LLM context overhead. Quorum executes graph discovery through a two-tier neurosymbolic pipeline: 1. Lexically Anchored Proposition Atomization ($\mathcal{V}_{\text{claim}}$): Two-pass atomic decomposition extracts candidate propositions grounded in verbatim citations satisfying physical provenance verification ($\lambda_i = 1$). 2. Sliding-Window Dependency Prediction ($\mathcal{E}_{\text{deps}}$): Propositions are partitioned into sequential chunk packets and scanned through a sliding window of width $W{\text{win}}$ chunks with step overlap $O_{\text{win}}$. Within each window, atoms are assigned deterministic, short attention anchors ($a_0, a_1$) to eliminate token bloat and prevent system UUID hallucinations. The model predicts directed causal dependencies ($X \xrightarrow{\mathbf{W}{XY}} Y$) along with explicit Toulmin backing warrants and expected precondition states. 3. Defensive Invariant Enforcement: Hallucinated or out-of-window aliases are dropped fail-fast by the service layer, immediate self-dependencies ($X \to X$) are pruned, and directed cycles are detected prior to wave execution via Johnson's elementary cycle algorithm in an isolated worker thread. If cycles are detected, participating nodes are flagged with cycle_detected = True, immediately clamping downstream argument cohesion to the Novice floor ($L_1$). 4. Sliding-Window Horizon Truncation of Long-Range Dependencies: Because proposition linking evaluates chunk packets within a finite sliding window ($W{\text{win}} = 4$ chunks, step overlap $O_{\text{win}} = 2$ chunks, $M_{\text{win}} \le 20$ atoms), dependency discovery operates within a localized observation horizon ($\Delta_{\max} \le M_{\text{win}} = 20$ proposition atoms). In extended policy and foresight documents ($|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \in [141, 252]$ atoms), long-range deductive dependencies linking foundational introductory axioms directly to terminal executive conclusions cannot co-occur in the same sliding window without continuous intermediate mentions. Consequently, foundational axioms lacking local descendants within their initial window remain at $\text{OutDegree}(v) = 0$, structurally contributing to the isolated concept ratio $r{\text{iso}}$.

================================================================================
Input  : Argument DAG G = (V, E, W), retracted node X in V, counterfactual state x* in {FAILED, BLOCKED},
         gate semantics mode in {CONJUNCTIVE_AND, DISJUNCTIVE_OR}
Output : Perturbed state mapping S*, Reachability Cascade Reach(X), Root Causes RC
================================================================================
Helper Functions:
  BlameParents(v, E_ref) := { P in Parents(v, E_ref) | S*[P] in {FAILED, BLOCKED} }
  expected_status(P', C) := EdgeCondition(P', C).expected_state (defaults to PASSED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1: S* <- InitialStateMap(V)                          // Base status from lexical grounding Gate
2: S*[X] <- x*                                       // Clamp intervened node to counterfactual state
3: Queue Q <- [ X ]
4: Visited Vis <- { X }
5: Cascade <- []
6: while Q is not empty do
7:     curr <- Q.pop_front()
8:     for each child C in Children(curr, E) do
9:         if C not in Vis then
10:            // Evaluate Precondition & Warrant Gate under active topology semantics:
11:            if mode == CONJUNCTIVE_AND then
12:                // Deductive Reachability Gate: All antecedents and supporting warrants must be valid
13:                survives_gate <- forall P' in Parents(C, E):
14:                                 (S*[P'] == expected_status(P', C) and lambda(W_{P'C}) == 1)
15:            else
16:                // Corroborative Warrant Redundancy: Alternative valid warrant shields C
17:                survives_gate <- exists P' in Parents(C, E)
18:                                 such that S*[P'] == PASSED and lambda(W_{P'C}) == 1
19:            end if
20:            if not survives_gate then
21:                S*[C] <- BLOCKED                      // (or N_A on precondition mismatch)
22:                Vis.add(C)
23:                Cascade.append(C)
24:                Q.push_back(C)
25:            end if
26:        end if
27:    end for
28: end while
29: RC <- { v in V | S*[v] in {FAILED, BLOCKED} and BlameParents(v, E) == emptyset }
30: return (S*, Cascade, RC)
================================================================================

    4.2.1. Pure Stateless Causal Cohesion Evaluation and the 0-Token Cumulative Guttman Invariant

    In contemporary multi-agent reasoning systems, assessing whether an authored discourse forms a coherent, non-trivial argument is routinely delegated to secondary LLM evaluation passes (e.g. LLM-as-a-Judge prompting with subjective instructions like "Rate the causal coherence from 1 to 5"; Zheng et al., 2023). This practice introduces stochastic scoring variance, circular hallucinations, token cost explosions, and prompt-injection vulnerability.

    Quorum eliminates stochastic judge prompts for argument cohesion through the Pure Stateless Causal Cohesion Evaluator, a deterministic compute engine that evaluates macro-argument structure directly from the topology of the mined Argument DAG $\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}} = (\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{deps}})$:

    1. The Cumulative Guttman Invariant and Multi-Factor Topological Predicates: Structural argument cohesion is formalized as a strictly cumulative, monotonic scale (Guttman, 1944) across Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale (BARS) Tiers 1–5 ($L_1 \subset L_2 \subset L_3 \subset L_4 \subset L_5$). To eliminate graph-theoretic degeneracies where fragmented subgraphs trivially pass degree-based filters, Quorum defines three orthogonal topological metrics over $\mathcal{G}_{\text{LAG}}$:

    2. Weakly Connected Components & Largest Component Ratio ($r_{\text{LCC}}$): Let $\text{WCC}(\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}}) = {\mathcal{C}_1, \dots, \mathcal{C}_k}$ be the set of weakly connected components in the underlying undirected projection of $\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}}$, with largest weakly connected component $\mathcal{V}{\text{LCC}} = \arg\max{\mathcal{C} \in \text{WCC}} |\mathcal{V}(\mathcal{C})|$. The macro-reachability component ratio is defined as: $$r_{\text{LCC}} = \begin{cases} 1.0, & |\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \le 1 \ \frac{|\mathcal{V}{\text{LCC}}|}{|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}|}, & |\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| > 1 \end{cases}$$

    3. Isolated Concept Fraction ($r_{\text{iso}}$): The fraction of propositions possessing degree zero ($\text{InDegree}(v) + \text{OutDegree}(v) = 0$): $$r_{\text{iso}} = \frac{|{v \in \mathcal{V}{\text{claim}} \mid \text{InDegree}(v) + \text{OutDegree}(v) = 0}|}{|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}|}$$
    4. Branch Convergence Ratio ($r_{\text{conv}}$): The fraction of claims synthesizing multi-premise warrants ($\text{InDegree}(v) \ge 2$), measuring convergent evidentiary branches: $$r_{\text{conv}} = \frac{|{v \in \mathcal{V}{\text{claim}} \mid \text{InDegree}(v) \ge 2}|}{|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}|}$$

    Dual-Track Evaluation Architecture (Continuous UI Telemetry vs. Discrete Guttman Invariants): Structural argument evaluation operates across two cleanly decoupled tracks: 1. Continuous UI Telemetry Scalar: For human reporting, dashboards, and Server-Driven UI (SDUI) diagnostic cards, structural logical cohesion is computed as a continuous scalar metric $\text{cohesion_score} \in [0, 1]$: $$\text{cohesion_score} = \begin{cases} 1.0, & |\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| = 0 \ \left(1.0 - r{\text{iso}}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{\mathbb{I}(\text{cycle_detected})}, & |\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| > 0 \end{cases}$$ where the factor $(1/2)^{\mathbb{I}(\text{cycle_detected})}$ visibly penalizes directed cycle defects in continuous dashboard telemetry, halving the displayed scalar score even when discrete grading is clamped to the Novice floor. 2. Discrete BARS Guttman Tier Invariants ($L_1 \dots L_5$): In contrast to continuous scalar reporting, discrete qualification across Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale (BARS) tiers is governed by strictly cumulative, monotonic Boolean predicates. Because directed acyclicity $(\neg \text{cycle_detected})$ is enforced as a strict prerequisite at Level 2 (and inherited monotonically by $L_3 \dots L_5$), $\mathbb{I}(\text{cycle_detected}) \equiv 0$ for all candidate acyclic graphs eligible to pass higher tiers, identically collapsing the scalar score to $\text{cohesion_score} \equiv 1.0 - r{\text{iso}}$. To eliminate algebraic redundancy between $\text{cohesion_score} \ge \theta_{Lk}$ and $r_{\text{iso}} \le r^{\max}{Lk}$ (where $r{\text{iso}} \le r^{\max}{Lk}$ strictly dominates $1.0 - r{\text{iso}} \ge \theta_{Lk}$), the hierarchical BARS predicates are formalized directly through non-redundant structural invariant bounds: $$\begin{aligned} L_1 &= (|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \ge 1) \quad (\text{Novice floor requires non-empty discourse graph}) \ L_2 &= L_1 \;\land\; (|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \ge 2) \;\land\; (\neg \text{cycle_detected}) \;\land\; (r_{\text{iso}} \le 1.0 - \theta_{L2}) \;\land\; (r_{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L2}) \ L_3 &= L_2 \;\land\; (|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \ge 5) \;\land\; (r_{\text{iso}} \le r^{\max}{L3}) \;\land\; (r{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L3}) \ L_4 &= L_3 \;\land\; (|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \ge 10) \;\land\; (r_{\text{iso}} \le r^{\max}{L4}) \;\land\; (r{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L4}) \;\land\; (r{\text{conv}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{conv}, L4}) \ L_5 &= L_4 \;\land\; (|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \ge 15) \;\land\; (r_{\text{iso}} \le r^{\max}{L5}) \;\land\; (r{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}_{\text{LCC}, L5}) \end{aligned} \tag{13}$$

    In the system configuration, thresholds are parameterized as: * $\theta_{L2} = 0.30 \implies 1.0 - \theta_{L2} = 0.70$ and $r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L2} = 0.35$ (minimum cohesion and component core for Level 2 emerging chains), * $r^{\max}{L3} = 0.20$ (baseline production default; satisfying $r^{\max}{L3} \le 1.0 - \theta{L3} = 0.50$, dominating continuous thresholding) and $r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L3} = 0.50$ (maximum 20% isolated claims and 50% majority component for Level 3 Toulmin warrant grounding; in exploratory cross-genre sensitivity analysis [Section 8.3], a calibrated configuration $r^{\max}{L3} = 0.30$ accommodates legitimate standalone scoping context [$\mathcal{V}{\text{scoping}}$] in strategic discourse where background framing premises lacking downstream causal edges [$\text{OutDegree}(v) = 0$] are counted in $r{\text{iso}}$ under the 0-token stateless evaluation law), * $r^{\max}{L4} = 0.15$ (satisfying $r^{\max}{L4} \le 1.0 - \theta_{L4} = 0.30$), $r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, L4} = 0.70$, and $r^{\min}{\text{conv}, L4} = 0.05$ (requiring branch convergence and multi-premise synthesis for Level 4), * $r^{\max}{L5} = 0.05$ (satisfying $r^{\max}{L5} \le 1.0 - \theta_{L5} = 0.15$) and $r^{\min}_{\text{LCC}, L5} = 0.85$ (maximum 5% isolated claims and 85% unified macro-reachability for Level 5 mastery).

    Graph-Theoretic Invariant Proof: Defense Against Dyad Fragmentation: We formalize why enforcing the orthogonal component threshold $r_{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, Lk}$ in Equation (13) is mathematically necessary. Suppose an authored text produces a completely fragmented discourse graph consisting of $k = n/2$ disjoint, unconnected pairs ($v_1 \to v_2, \; v_3 \to v_4, \dots$). Under a naive degree-zero formulation ($1 - r{\text{iso}}$), every node possesses $\text{deg}(v) = 1 > 0$, yielding $r_{\text{iso}} = 0.0$ and $\mathbb{I}(\text{cycle_detected}) = 0$. Consequently, a collection of disconnected two-node islands would trivially achieve a perfect raw $\text{cohesion_score} = 1.00$ and potentially pass higher tiers if cohesion were evaluated without reachability gates, despite possessing zero global reachability, zero macro-coherence, and zero multi-premise synthesis. By enforcing $r_{\text{LCC}} \ge r^{\min}{\text{LCC}, Lk}$ as an independent Boolean conjunct at every level $L_2 \dots L_5$, the largest component size is bounded by $|\mathcal{V}{\text{LCC}}| = 2$, yielding $r_{\text{LCC}} = 2/n \to 0$ as $n$ grows. For any non-trivial discourse ($n \ge 6$), $r_{\text{LCC}} \le 0.33 < r^{\min}_{\text{LCC}, L2} = 0.35$, which immediately fails Level 2 ($L_2 = \text{False}$) and deterministically clamps the fragmented discourse to the Novice floor ($L_1$), proving absolute mathematical defense against dyad fragmentation without double-penalizing component size in the scalar cohesion score.

    Decoupling of Baseline Guttman Scoring from Perturbation Dynamics: We explicitly formalize the mathematical decoupling between baseline BARS scoring (Equation 13) and counterfactual ATMS surgery (Algorithm 1). Evaluating authored discourse quality requires measuring the structural cohesion of the unperturbed argument graph $\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}}$ as delivered by the author.     8.6. Limitations and Empirical Generalization Agenda

    While the observed inter-run reliability ($\bar{\kappa} = 0.9348$) provides strong empirical support for Quorum’s neurosymbolic architecture, we emphasize the specific limitations of this preliminary validation: 1. Limited Sample Cardinality and Multi-Level Clustering Hierarchy: The quantitative results reflect exactly two pairwise evaluation trials ($T = 2$ paired trials, 4 full workflow runs). In evaluating statistical confidence, clustering operates at two distinct hierarchical tiers: (i) Meso-level clustering (the step/batch level, where multiple criteria share the same LLM decoding context and document sections) is explicitly modeled and bounded via the non-parametric cluster bootstrap ($K = 10$ step clusters across trials, yielding $\text{SE}{\text{cluster}} = 0.0275$ and 95% CI $[0.8784, 0.9868]$ in Table 2). In the offline component ablation benchmark (Table 7), where error rates are stratified uniformly across steps, meso-level clustering is evaluated via two-stage hierarchical resampling (Stage 1: step cluster resampling; Stage 2: within-cluster atom resampling) with a conservative truncation floor ($\text{SE} \ge \text{SE}{\text{nominal}}$) to prevent single-stage bootstrap contraction artifacts under small $K$ and $\rho_{\text{ICC}} \le 0$. (ii) Conversely, macro-level clustering (the document level, $T = 2$) cannot be non-parametrically bootstrapped because resampling from an empirical support of only two document clusters yields a mathematically degenerate distribution of only three discrete configurations (Cameron & Miller, 2015). Consequently, while inter-trial independence is preserved via variance pooling, establishing population-level asymptotic bounds over the document universe strictly requires expanding to $T \ge 20$ paired trials across diverse organizational, legal, and technical document genres. 2. Single Foundation Model Family: The trials were executed using Google Gemini 3.8 Flash (gemini-3.8-flash; Google DeepMind, 2026). While the Two-Tier Verification Architecture is provider-agnostic, cross-model inter-rater reliability across distinct model architectures (e.g., Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, OpenAI GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3) must be systematically quantified to measure inter-provider epistemic alignment. 3. Task Domain and Genre Generalization: While our empirical trials validate human–AI interaction across both operational organizational policy reform (the Remote Work policy case) and executive strategic foresight (the Strategic Foresight / Sitra case), broader empirical generalization requires extending this evaluation protocol to formal legal contracts, clinical protocol verification, and automated software architecture documentation. 4. Construct Validity vs. Algorithmic Reliability and Semantic Error Calibration: The empirical trials establish high algorithmic test–retest reproducibility ($\bar{\kappa} = 0.9348$) under stochastic frontier sampling, suppressing aleatoric token decoding noise. However, in classical psychometrics and measurement theory, reliability is a necessary but strictly insufficient condition for construct validity: an automated evaluator can execute with perfect deterministic consistency while embedding systematic inductive biases. While Quorum mathematically guarantees physical citation provenance ($\lambda \in {0, 1}$), evaluating the factual accuracy of semantic entailment ($\mathcal{C}i \models \tau_i$) requires empirical validation against external human judgment. Specifically, automated discourse evaluation exhibits three distinct diagnostic error dimensions: (a) Type I Semantic Misattribution ($\beta_{\text{sem}}$): where a positive citation is verified as physically present but fails to semantically entail the rubric hypothesis (empirically bounded in our pilot audit to $\hat{\beta}{\text{sem}} = 0.0250$, 95% Wilson score CI $[0.0044, 0.1288]$ across $N = 40$ passed atoms); (b) Type II Firewall Rejection Penalty ($\text{FNR}_{\text{rejection}}$): where the LLM sensor extracted an unanchored candidate quote for a genuine author competency that was rejected by the lexical firewall due to paraphrasing ($27.59\%$, $8/29$ in Ablation Configuration 2); and (c) Type II Sensor Omission Blind Spot ($\text{FNR}_{\text{omission}}$): where the LLM sensor committed a complete retrieval failure ($\omega(a_i) = \text{FAILED}, \mathcal{C}i = \emptyset$) on a capability criterion that the human author actually demonstrated in the source deliverable. To empirically estimate the system-wide False Negative Rate across failed decisions, we conducted a blinded, stratified pilot audit of $N = 30$ randomly sampled failed positive capability criteria ($\omega(a_i) = \text{FAILED}, \lambda_i = 0$) across Trial Pairs A and B (stratified across BARS Levels 1–5). Manual human expert verification confirmed that $26 / 30$ ($86.67\%$, 95% Wilson CI $[70.32\%, 94.69\%]$) were Confirmed True Negatives ($TN$), where the source texts genuinely omitted the required analytical structures (e.g., formal Bayesian uncertainty intervals, complete Toulmin hexads with empirical backings and rebuttal conditions, or cognitive bias falsification tests). Only $4 / 30$ ($13.33\%$, 95% Wilson CI $[5.31\%, 29.68\%]$) were categorized as False Negatives ($FN$): $1 / 30$ ($3.33\%$) arose from lexical firewall rejection of candidate quotes due to minor inflectional or punctuation variance ($\text{FNR}{\text{rejection}}$), while $3 / 30$ ($10.00\%$) arose from sensor retrieval omissions on diffuse multi-sentence context ($\text{FNR}{\text{omission}}$). This confirms that over $86\%$ of negative capability evaluations reflect genuine authorial omission rather than verbatim quotation rejection, bounding the estimated False Negative Rate on failed atoms to $\widehat{\text{FNR}}{\text{failed}} \approx 13.3\%$. Consequently, while the platform's high precision ($\text{PPV} \approx 97.5\%$) and physical specificity ($\text{Specificity}{\text{lex}} = 1.000$) are empirically established, population-level semantic calibration and inter-annotator concordance ($\kappa{\text{human-Quorum}}$) across heterogeneous document genres will be formally quantified by our planned full-matrix multi-rater study ($N = 110$). 5. Micro-Structural Graph Variance vs. Macro-Topological Stability (Resolution of the ATMS Surgery Paradox): In natural language discourse, exact graph isomorphism ($\mathcal{G}1 \simeq \mathcal{G}_2$, with edge Jaccard similarity $J_E = 1.0$) across stochastic sampling runs is neither achievable nor ontologically meaningful, as autoregressive decoders generate slight lexical paraphrases when isolating proposition boundaries and citations (observed verbatim quote Jaccard overlap was $25.3\%–31.9\%$, while semantic claim text overlap was $2.5\%–7.2\%$). Crucially, this contextualizes the deterministic guarantees of Algorithm 1 (Section 4.2) and Property 2 (Section 6): discrete ATMS premise surgery and root-cause sets $RC$ are mathematically deterministic conditional on a single compiled graph instance $\mathcal{G}{\text{LAG}}$, providing rigorous local explainability within that execution run. However, because independent runs yield non-isomorphic micro-graphs, cross-run test–retest reproducibility cannot and does not rely on node- or edge-isomorphism. Rather, Quorum establishes the Two-Pillar Causal Architecture: (i) baseline evaluative reproducibility ($\bar{\kappa} = 0.9348$) is achieved via Macro-Topological Invariant Stability, where the Cumulative Guttman Invariant (Section 4.2.1, Equation 13) functions as a robust topological low-pass filter over the unperturbed base graph; while (ii) Algorithm 1 operates as a decoupled Interactive Diagnostic & Counterfactual Surgery Engine (Table 4b) that isolates root causes ($|RC| = 1$), evaluates warrant independence, drives prescriptive remediation (actionable premise repair recommendations), and dampens secondary cascading fault penalties (fair fault-attribution scoring breakdowns). This macro-stability was corroborated across both the Remote Work policy case (cohesion delta $+0.0010$, isolated ratio delta $0.10\%$) and the Strategic Foresight / Sitra case (cohesion delta $+0.0299$). Future research will formalize graph-edit distance (GED) bounds and kernel-based graph similarity metrics to systematically quantify micro-structural variance across model architectures. 6. In-Silico Component Ablation vs. Direct Live-Ablation Re-runs: While Configuration 1 in the component ablation study incorporates physical execution trace baselines, Configurations 2–5 and the transient fault stress test evaluate system defenses via a standardized offline benchmark harness. This harness injects controlled perturbations—including candidate citations evaluated against a frozen diagnostic pool of 29 candidate chimera citations (19.7% candidate attack pressure) empirically harvested from unconstrained frontier LLM decodes (21 synthetic hallucinations, 8 paraphrased retrieval gaps), simulated 0.9% API drops, and prompt structure alterations—across the identical 220 atom frames to isolate marginal component contributions without incurring the multi-thousand-dollar API expenditure of running full live factorial re-executions across all 5 configurations. While the harness faithfully models the production verification pipeline and reproduces the observed inter-run reliability ($\kappa = 0.9453$ in harness vs. $\bar{\kappa} = 0.9348$ in physical trials), future multi-site empirical trials should evaluate full-scale live-API factorial ablations across external foundation models. 7. Sliding-Window Horizon Truncation of Macro-Discourse Dependencies: In Definition 3 and the empirical trials, proposition linking ($\Phi_{\text{link}}$) is evaluated within local sliding windows ($W_{\text{win}} = 4$ chunks, $O_{\text{win}} = 2$ chunks, $M_{\text{win}} \le 20$ proposition atoms). In extended strategic documents ($|\mathcal{V}{\text{claim}}| \in [141, 252]$ atoms), the maximum observable dependency distance is bounded by $\Delta{\max} \approx 2 \cdot W_{\text{win}}$ chunks ($\le 20$ atoms). Consequently, foundational scoping axioms stated in early introductory sections and final executive recommendations synthesized in concluding sections never co-occur within the same sliding-window attention scope. Unless intermediate claims in every intervening chunk redundantly reiterate the introductory premise, long-range deductive edges cannot be formed. This horizon truncation structurally forces foundational axioms to remain at $\text{OutDegree}(v) = 0$, directly driving the observed isolated concept ratio ($r_{\text{iso}} = 52.4\%$ in Pair A, $23.6\%–26.6\%$ in Pair B) and necessitating the empirical relaxation of the production threshold to $r^{\max}_{L3} = 0.30$. In future iterations, this structural limitation will be resolved by pinning identified root axioms ($\text{InDegree}(v) = 0$) into a persistent Global Context Anchor header prepended across all subsequent sliding windows.

    9.1. Comparison with Reasoning and Orchestration Frameworks

    • Chain-of-Thought (CoT) (Wei et al., 2022) and Self-Consistency (Wang et al., 2023): CoT relies entirely on the autoregressive distribution to maintain reasoning consistency, causing early errors to cascade exponentially (Equation 2). Self-Consistency attempts to mitigate this by sampling multiple CoT paths from a single model and taking a majority vote. However, as proven in Equation (25), single-model sampling under correlated epistemic priors ($\bar{\rho}_{\text{intra}} \approx 1$) merely amplifies systematic hallucinations when $p > 0.5$. Quorum restricts ensembling to aleatoric jitter suppression while enforcing epistemic soundness via non-generative symbolic invariants ($\lambda \in {0, 1}$, $\pi = -1$, and ATMS graph surgery).
    • Tree of Thoughts (ToT) (Yao et al., 2023b) and Graph of Thoughts (GoT) (Besta et al., 2024): ToT and GoT structure thoughts into search graphs (BFS/DFS). However, their nodes remain unstructured natural language strings, and state pruning relies on LLM self-evaluation. Quorum’s nodes are strictly typed computational operators producing validated structured records, and state termination is mathematically guaranteed.
    • ReAct (Yao et al., 2023a) and Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023): Reflexion implements heuristic self-correction loops. However, recent empirical work (Huang et al., 2024) proves that LLMs cannot reliably self-correct without external deterministic ground truth. Quorum replaces circular self-reflection with external, asymmetric Popperian falsification.
    • Declarative Pipeline Compilers (DSPy) (Khattab et al., 2024): DSPy optimizes declarative prompt modules through automated parameter tuning and teleprompter demonstrations. While DSPy optimizes pipeline prompt strings, its execution DAG lacks formal truth maintenance or discrete causal graph surgery. Quorum structures orchestration into Kahn topological waves over typed schema invariants with zero prompt-scraping or unvalidated string handoffs.

    10. Limitations and Practical Considerations

    1. Computational Overhead, FinOps Allocation, and Heterogeneous Multi-Model Scaling: Parallel ensemble verification and deterministic lexical verification (Architectural Property 1) increase inference token consumption relative to unverified single-pass sampling. In Quorum, FinOps resource allocation, verification rigor, and architectural scaling are governed by a partitioned strategy:
    2. Operational DAG Execution (Aleatoric Stabilization): In baseline execution, high-entropy proposition atoms are evaluated using a single-pass Best-of-3 ensemble executed with the step's assigned model configuration, dispatching parallel calls via asynchronous coroutines with majority voting ($k \ge 2$ of $3$) and Popperian Null Hypothesis tie-breaking. This acts strictly as an aleatoric low-pass filter to dampen stochastic token decoding variance around decision thresholds under non-zero temperature. To prevent combinatorial token explosions, atoms are evaluated in batched sensor payloads, resulting in only 5–15 discrete LLM calls per full workflow execution (costing approximately $1.80–$3.05 per run in frontier flash models). Furthermore, Argument Causal Cohesion (A-SCM) is evaluated under a 0-token computational contract in pure graph logic, avoiding speculative counterfactual branch expansions.
    3. Offline Cross-Model Variance Benchmarking: Comprehensive multi-provider parameter stability and alignment testing across diverse foundation model families (including Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, and DeepSeek) is conducted in dedicated offline evaluation workers, establishing baseline cross-architecture divergence metrics without burdening real-time execution pipelines.
    4. Simultaneous Heterogeneous Multi-Model Production Roadmap: In actual production deployment, the platform will actively benchmark simultaneous heterogeneous multi-model ensembling within live execution waves. Rather than querying the same foundation model three times (which limits epistemic diversity against systematic pretraining blindspots as formalized in Section 6), the live voting branches will be concurrently routed across independent model families (specifically Google Gemini 3.8 Flash [gemini-3.8-flash; Google DeepMind, 2026], Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and OpenAI GPT-4o). By tapping into distinct pretraining distributions, tokenizers, and alignment philosophies ($\bar{\rho}{\text{inter}} \ll 1$), this multi-provider deployment expands the effective ensemble sample size $M{\text{eff}}$ per Equation (25). The production trial will empirically benchmark whether the resulting gains in epistemic robustness justify the associated wall-clock synchronization latency (bounded by the slowest provider), cross-API failure modes, and heterogeneous FinOps token costs.
    5. Exogenous Axiom Misattribution and Provenance Friction: In sparse strategic corpora, separating genuine exogenous axioms (primitive operational constraints requiring no internal textual ancestors) from unanchored orphan claims relies on explicit document bounding. If an author introduces implicit domain premises or industry-standard baseline axioms without physical citations in the source text, the closed-world lexical null hypothesis flags them as unverified orphans ($\lambda_i = 0$). While this behavior mathematically adheres to Toulmin's requirement for explicit evidentiary warrants, it introduces provenance friction in domains where shared common-sense assumptions are standard practice, necessitating explicit axiomatic white-listing or input provenance declaration.
    6. Rigid BARS Scoring and Perceived Excess Punitive Calibration: Calibrated BARS power-curve damping ($\gamma = 1.25 \dots 1.5071$, corresponding to $\zeta \in [50, 70]$) yields conservative capability scores under enterprise zero-trust rubrics. While our empirical production trials executed at baseline strictness $\zeta = 0$ ($\gamma = 1.00$) to evaluate unattenuated raw model behavior, deploying non-linear damping in live organizational environments can lead human operators to perceive score compression at lower tiers as excessively punitive without peer normalization. In the empirical paired trials across both human–AI evaluation cases (the Remote Work policy case and the Strategic Foresight / Sitra case), normalized matrix scores ranged from $0.0\%$ to $61.9\%$ with stable inter-run consistency (MAD $3.05–3.99$ points). To remain interpretable across diverse user populations, raw BARS scores require supplementary cohort percentile calibration that contextualizes diagnostic results against normalized institutional baselines.
    7. The Enthymeme Boundary and the Semantic Entailment Gap: Natural discourse is inherently pragmatic, elliptical, and enthymematic, frequently omitting unstated common-sense warrants. Quorum explicitly acknowledges that it does not discover objective physical causality or achieve universal automated semantic proof over arbitrary text, as determining general semantic entailment without human supervision is bounded by the semantic entailment gap. The platform's deterministic guarantees are strictly confined to physical citation provenance ($\lambda \in {0, 1}$); residual semantic entailment is bounded stochastically by role-asymmetric Popperian falsification ($\beta_{\text{sem}}$) and isolated through discrete Assumption-Based Truth Maintenance System (ATMS) reachability pruning.
    8. Vulnerability to the "Illusion of Control" via Prompt Sanitization: As demonstrated across both empirical audits (the Remote Work policy trial and the Strategic Foresight / Sitra trial), human users may operate under the cognitive assumption that formulating concise, jargon-free prompts guarantees analytically rigorous output. However, this clean surface form (e.g., zero mechanical phrase load) can mask unreflective routine delegation wherein the AI's first associative draft is accepted without counterfactual testing, boundary specification, or trade-off evaluation. While Quorum's Human Agency & Collaboration Matrix ($\mathcal{M}{\text{HACM}}$) and Agency-to-Outcome Divergence ($\Delta{\text{agency-outcome}}$) explicitly diagnose this form-to-content disparity, automated diagnostic evaluation cannot substitute for the operator's active willingness to engage in critical cognitive steering.
    9. Episodic Identifier Aliasing, Attention Economics, and Fail-Fast State Hydration: While Section 2.1 formalizes the bijective mapping $\mu: \mathcal{I}{\text{UUID}} \longleftrightarrow \mathcal{V}{\text{alias}}$, maintaining distributed referential integrity requires strict lifecycle isolation. In Quorum, alias mappings are strictly scoped per execution instance to prevent cross-tenant state pollution. When execution is distributed across asynchronous worker nodes, the mapping state is serialized into an immutable manifest transported within execution metadata. The worker reconstitutes the bijection and executes strict fail-fast validation: any hallucinated alias matching an active prefix that does not exist in the manifest immediately halts execution with an unrecoverable validation error, preventing corrupted foreign keys from entering persistent graph state.

    Appendix A: Statistical Methodology & Schema Accounting

    A.1. Two-Stage Hierarchical Cluster Bootstrap and Finite-Sample Bounds

    In high-stakes natural language evaluation, proposition atoms evaluated within the same workflow step or document section are not identically and independently distributed (i.i.d.): they share common prompt contexts, common foundation model key-value attention caches, and common latent document representations. In standard biostatistical and psychometric literature, naive application of Cohen's asymptotic variance formula: $$\text{Var}{\text{nominal}}(\hat{\kappa}) = \frac{P_o(1 - P_o)}{N (1 - P_e)^2}$$ underestimates the true standard error when positive intra-cluster correlation exists ($\rho{\text{ICC}} > 0$), inducing pseudoreplication bias and artificially narrow confidence intervals.

    To account for batch-level clustering across our empirical evaluations without imposing parametric distribution assumptions, Quorum implements a Hierarchical Two-Stage Non-Parametric Cluster Bootstrap:

    1. Stage 1 (Meso-Level Cluster Resampling): Let $\mathcal{K} = {C_1, C_2, \dots, C_K}$ denote the set of $K = 10$ distinct matrix step execution clusters across trials ($K = 5$ clusters per trial: $\mathcal{M}{\text{Toulmin}}, \mathcal{M}{\text{Causal}}, \mathcal{M}{\text{Transp}}, \mathcal{M}{\text{HACM}}, \mathcal{M}{\text{Bloom}}$). In each bootstrap iteration $b \in {1, \dots, B}$ ($B = 2,000$ to $10,000$), we draw $K$ step clusters with replacement: $$\mathcal{K}^{(b)} = {C{k_1}, C_{k_2}, \dots, C_{k_K}}, \quad k_j \sim \text{Uniform}({1, \dots, K})$$
    2. Stage 2 (Within-Cluster Atom Resampling): For each selected cluster $C \in \mathcal{K}^{(b)}$ containing $N_C$ proposition criteria, we resample $N_C$ atom evaluation frames with replacement: $$\mathcal{A}C^{(b)} = {a{i_1}, \dots, a_{i_{N_C}}}, \quad i_j \sim \text{Uniform}({1, \dots, N_C})$$
    3. Empirical Percentile Confidence Intervals: For each resampled replicate dataset $\mathcal{D}^{(b)} = \bigcup_{C \in \mathcal{K}^{(b)}} \mathcal{A}C^{(b)}$, we recompute the evaluation concordance $\kappa^{(b)}$. Across $B$ bootstrap realizations, the 95% confidence interval is extracted non-parametrically using empirical percentiles: $$\text{CI}{95\%} = \left[ \hat{\kappa}_{0.025}^, \; \hat{\kappa}_{0.975}^ \right]$$ Empirical percentile intervals preserve the natural parameter boundaries $[-1.0, 1.0]$ and naturally accommodate distribution skewness near the upper boundary ($\kappa \to 1.0$), avoiding standard normal approximation clipping artifacts.
    4. Conservative Truncation Floor: Standard single-stage cluster resampling artificially contracts confidence intervals when error rates are stratified uniformly across clusters ($\rho_{\text{ICC}} \le 0$), as between-cluster variance vanishes while within-cluster binomial sampling variance is omitted. By combining Stage 1 and Stage 2 resampling with the conservative truncation floor: $$\text{SE}{\text{conservative}} = \max\left(\text{SE}{\text{nominal}}, \; \text{SE}_{\text{cluster}}\right)$$ Quorum restores finite-sample binomial degrees of freedom, yielding Kish Design Effect standard error inflations of $\text{Deff}^{1/2} \in [1.10\times, 1.37\times]$ across all configurations while proving that lower bounds remain strictly within the Almost Perfect Agreement tier ($> 0.81$).
    5. Discrete Granularity and Finite-Cluster Ceiling Effect ($K = 5$ per Trial): When evaluating trial-specific bootstrap confidence intervals on a per-pair basis, each trial comprises $K = 5$ matrix step clusters ($N = 110$ criteria per trial; $K = 10$ across both pooled trials). In Trial Pair B (Table 3), 3 of the 5 matrices ($\mathcal{M}{\text{Toulmin}}$, $\mathcal{M}{\text{Causal}}$, and $\mathcal{M}{\text{Transp}}$) exhibited zero disagreements across runs ($P_o = 100\%$). Consequently, in a non-parametric bootstrap over $K = 5$ clusters, the probability that all 5 resampled clusters in Stage 1 are drawn exclusively from these 3 perfect-agreement matrices is at least: $$P(\text{All 5 drawn clusters have 0 disagreements}) = \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^5 = 0.07776 \approx 7.8\%$$ Additionally, even in Stage 1 draws that include one of the 2 matrices containing disagreements (totaling only 2 discordant atoms across the entire trial), Stage 2 within-cluster atom resampling with replacement has a non-negligible probability of missing the discordant atoms entirely. In all such resampled realizations ($> 7.8\%$ of total bootstrap iterations), the resampled dataset contains zero disagreements ($P_o^{(b)} = 1.0$, $P_e^{(b)} < 1.0$), yielding exact concordance $\kappa^{(b)} = 1.0000$. Because this point-mass probability at $1.0000$ exceeds the upper tail alpha cutoff ($7.8\% > 2.5\%$), the non-parametric 97.5th empirical percentile is discretely clipped at the mathematical ceiling $\hat{\kappa}{0.975}^ = 1.0000$ (yielding $[0.8946, 1.0000]$ for Pair B, and analogously $[0.8212, 1.0000]$ for Pair A where 5 disagreements are concentrated across 2 matrices). Crucially, when pooling across both trials ($K = 10$ clusters, $N = 220$ criteria), the combinatorial probability of drawing 10 zero-disagreement clusters contracts to $(3/10)^{10} \approx 5.9 \times 10^{-6} \ll 2.5\%$, eliminating the discrete ceiling truncation and yielding a smooth, interior upper percentile bound of $\hat{\kappa}_{0.975}^ = 0.9868$ ($[0.8784, 0.9868]$).
