We are developing a backend DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph) orchestration system that processes and analyzes large documents using concurrent LLM steps. The default 5 RPM limit severely bottlenecks our workflow execution, as our architecture is designed to evaluate multiple data chunks in parallel to reduce latency. Increasing the limit to 60 RPM will allow our orchestrator to run 3 to 5 concurrent requests smoothly without hitting Quota Exceeded (429) errors during our development and testing phase in the europe-north1 region.
